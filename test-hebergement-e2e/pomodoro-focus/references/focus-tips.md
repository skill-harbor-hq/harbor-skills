# Focus tips

Break suggestions to offer between pomodoro blocks:

- Stand up and look at something far away for a minute.
- Drink a glass of water.
- Write down, in one line, where the work stopped and what the next step is.
  This makes restarting the next block much easier.
- Do not open feeds or inboxes during a 5-minute break; they eat the next
  block.

If the user derails twice in a row, suggest a shorter block (15 minutes)
instead of a longer one.
